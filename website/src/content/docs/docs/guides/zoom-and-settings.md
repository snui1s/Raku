---
title: Zoom and settings
description: Zoom the paper, change its width, and scale the interface.
---

Open **Settings** from the gear button in the header.

## Zoom the paper

Hold `Ctrl` and scroll the mouse wheel over the note to zoom the paper. These shortcuts work too:

| Shortcut | Action |
|---|---|
| `Ctrl` + `+` | Zoom in |
| `Ctrl` + `-` | Zoom out |
| `Ctrl` + `0` | Reset zoom to 100% |

The zoom range is 50% to 250%. Zoom only changes the paper. The interface around it stays the same size.

## Settings

**Paper**

- **Zoom:** the same value as `Ctrl` + scroll, with a reset button.
- **Width:** Narrow, Medium, Wide or Full.
- **Line spacing:** Compact, Normal or Relaxed.

**Writing**

- **Auto-save after:** 1, 2 or 5 seconds after you stop typing.
- **Spell check:** off by default.
- **Show folder and edit time:** the small row above the note title.

**Appearance**

- **Interface scale:** 50%, 75%, 100% or 125%. This scales the sidebar, header and menus, not the paper.
- **Dark mode:** switches between light and dark. The color palette is chosen from the header.
- **Reduce animations:** turns off transitions. Raku also respects the reduced-motion setting of Windows.

Settings are stored on your computer and kept between launches.

## Focus mode

Press `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `Z` for **Zen mode**, which hides the sidebar and header. Press `Esc` to leave it. The header also has a Pomodoro timer.
