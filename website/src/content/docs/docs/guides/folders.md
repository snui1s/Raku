---
title: Folders and drag and drop
description: Organize notes into folders, pin the important ones, and move notes by dragging.
---

The sidebar groups your notes into **Pinned**, **Folders** and **Inbox**. A note that is not in a folder lives in the Inbox.

## Create and manage folders

- Click the **New folder** button in the sidebar, type a name, and press `Enter`.
- Use the **New note in folder** button on a folder to create a note directly inside it.
- Use **Rename folder** to rename it. Folders have a color, shown as a dot next to the name.
- Click a folder to expand or collapse it. Hovering over a collapsed folder while dragging a note expands it.
- Use **Delete folder** to remove a folder (click again to confirm). Its notes are not deleted: they move to the Inbox.

## Move notes

Drag a note onto a folder to move it there. Drag it onto the Inbox to take it out of a folder. The folder under your pointer lights up while you drag.

You can also use the **Move to folder** button on a note if you prefer clicking to dragging.

## Pin, rename and delete

Hover over a note in the sidebar to see its buttons:

- **Pin to top** moves it to the Pinned section. Click again to unpin.
- **Rename** sets a title by hand. Otherwise the title comes from the first line of the note.
- **Delete** removes the note.

:::caution
Deleting a note cannot be undone. Make a [backup](/docs/guides/export-and-backup/) first if you are unsure.
:::
